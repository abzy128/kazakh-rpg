# Customs the game master judges

Setting: an 18th-century Kazakh aul at its autumn camp (күзеу) on the day of the first snow. No Russian Empire, no modern objects, no tea samovars; drinks are water, milk, айран, and the last of the autumn қымыз. Meat comes from the autumn slaughter (соғым).

## Greeting (сәлем)

- The younger person greets the elder first; a host greets an arriving rider first. "Ассалаумағалейкүм" (to men, formal) is answered "Уағалейкүмассалам". "Сәлеметсіз бе" is a polite general greeting.
- Greeting with both hands, or right hand on the heart, shows respect. Ignoring a guest, or making him greet first, is rude.

## Receiving a rider

- Run out to meet the rider, hold his horse's bridle while he dismounts, and tie the horse at the hitching rope (керме) or lead it to shelter. In a storm, caring for the guest's horse matters as much as the guest.
- An unexpected traveler is a "Құдайы қонақ" — a guest sent by God. He must be received, fed and sheltered, whoever he is. Turning him away shames the household.

## Inside the yurt

- The guest is invited in first. He is seated at the төр — the place of honor opposite the door, behind the hearth. The place by the door (босаға) is for the lowest in rank; seating a guest there is an insult.
- Do not step on the threshold. Do not walk between the hearth and the guest.

## The meal (қонақасы)

- Қонақасы, the guest's meal, is his right. First offer water to wash the hands (a jug and basin), then drinks, then the meal.
- Do not ask the guest about his business, his road or his news before he has eaten. First feed, then ask.
- When meat is served, the boiled sheep's head (бас) is offered to the most honored guest (бас тарту). He cuts pieces and shares them with others at the table — giving the ear to the youngest ("listen to your elders") and the palate to a girl, by tradition.
- After the meal the guest or the eldest gives a blessing (бата) with open palms; everyone says "Әумин" and passes hands over the face.

## Seeing off (шығарып салу)

- The guest is accompanied out to his horse and helped to mount. Wishing a good road: "Жолың болсын".

## Weather and herds

- Signs of a coming blizzard (боран): a ring around the sun or moon, horses gathering with tails to the wind and refusing to graze, sudden quiet of birds, a heavy grey wall in the north-east.
- Horses (жылқы) graze far out; in a storm a herd drifts downwind and can be lost. Bring them in before the storm; never gallop straight at a herd — circle wide and push gently from behind.

## Eagle hunting (бүркітшілік)

- Қансонар: the first fresh snow, when tracks are clear — the best time to hunt.
- The golden eagle (бүркіт) rides hooded (томаға) on the hunter's right arm, which rests on a forked prop (бүркітшінің таяғы / балдақ).
- Approach the fox slowly, from downwind, and from higher ground. Galloping or noise makes the fox bolt. Unhood the eagle only when the fox is in sight, then release.
- The catch is shared; the first catch of the season is a reason to invite neighbors. A hunter respects the eagle as a family member and releases it back to the wild after years of service.
